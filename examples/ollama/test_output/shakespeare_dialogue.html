Act I, Scene I. Verona. A public place. Enter SAMPSON and GREGORY, armed with swords and bucklers. SAMPSON: Gregory, on my word, we'll not carry coals. GREGORY: No, for then we should be colliers. SAMPSON: I mean, an we be in choler, we'll draw. GREGORY: Ay, while you live, draw your neck out of collar. Enter ABRAHAM and BALTHASAR. ABRAHAM: Do you bite your thumb at us, sir? SAMPSON: I do bite my thumb, sir. ABRAHAM: Do you bite your thumb at us, sir? SAMPSON: No, sir, I do not bite my thumb at you, sir, but I bite my thumb, sir. GREGORY: Do you quarrel, sir? ABRAHAM: Quarrel, sir? No, sir. Enter BENVOLIO. BENVOLIO: Part, fools! Put up your swords. You know not what you do. Enter TYBALT. TYBALT: What, art thou drawn among these heartless hinds? Turn thee, Benvolio; look upon thy death. BENVOLIO: I do but keep the peace. Put up thy sword, Or manage it to part these men with me. TYBALT: What, drawn, and talk of peace? I hate the word, As I hate hell, all Montagues, and thee. Have at thee, coward!
